# Corrections and removal requests

## How to ask

Use the form: **https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdLmqvOsFA35UOqQTrvfmqp5wvkaI1RR33N4m_A2jH8Va8FVA/viewform**

It asks:
- what the request is about;
- the record ID or name;
- what should change and why;
- a supporting source;
- whether you are the person or organization listed;
- an optional email address for a reply.

The email is used only to reply and, for removal requests, to confirm the request comes from the person listed.

## How requests are decided

- **Factual corrections.** A correction is accepted when a public record, filing, official page or reliable report supports it. The record is changed, not annotated, and the source is added to `source_urls`. A request without a source is checked against the record's existing sources; if they do not support the contested detail, the detail is removed.
- **Positions (stance).** A stance changes when the actor's own statement, vote, sponsorship or testimony shows a different or more recent position. A person may state their current position, and the record is updated with that statement, dated.
- **Removal requests from private individuals.** People listed by role only, such as volunteers, witnesses and registered lobbyists, are private individuals. If one of them asks for their listing to be removed, the listing is withheld from the public directory. They do not have to show an error. The record is kept privately so that the removal stays in effect if the data is rebuilt.
- **Removal requests from public figures and organizations.** Records of public officials, organization officers, authors and organizations are kept when the sources support them, because their Article V roles were public. Errors in those records are corrected as above.
- **Missing records.** A missing record is added only if a primary source documents a direct Article V role.

## How each change is recorded

Every accepted change is made in the private master database. It is never made by hand-editing the public table. Each change gets an entry in two places:
- **The private database:** a row in its corrections file, whose note cites the request date, the evidence and the reason.
- **The public change log:** a dated entry in `CHANGELOG.md`, giving the record ID and what changed. The requester is not named.

Withheld listings appear in the change log as "listing withheld at the request of the person listed". The public table is then regenerated by script, which reapplies the privacy checks.
