# Wikipedia attribution

Some rows in these tables were adapted from English Wikipedia articles:
- `data/ledger.csv`
- `population.csv`
- `ratifications_01_13.csv`
- `ratifications_14_27.csv`
- `ratifications_unratified.csv`
- `states.csv`
- `votes_cast.csv`
- `boundaries.csv`
- `legislature_sizes.csv`
- `ratification_margins.csv`
- `secession.csv`
- `amendments_meta.csv`

Each row taken from Wikipedia names its article in the `source_url` column.

- **Source:** Wikipedia, The Free Encyclopedia, by Wikipedia contributors. Each article's contributors are listed in its history at the URL given in the row.
- **License:** Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/.
- **Changes made:** values were extracted into tables, names and dates normalized, rows matched to states and amendments, and some rows merged with or checked against other sources (cited per row).

Because of the ShareAlike term, these twelve tables are distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0. Rows are being re-sourced to primary records over time; a table whose rows no longer come from Wikipedia can move to CC BY 4.0.
